## 0.1.0

First release.

- Browse any folder on your computer or remote host as a list or as a tree, not only the folders in your workspace.
- Show the size and date modified of each file, with sortable and resizable columns.
- Filter the files in the view.
- Open, rename, duplicate, and delete files and folders, copy paths, and make new files and folders.
- Update the view automatically when files are created or deleted.
- Turn the view on or off with the `fileNavigator.enabled` setting.
